# Tacos al Pastor
- Cuisine Type: Mexican
- Preparation Time: 20 mins (plus 2 hours marinating)
- Cooking Time: 15 mins
- Serving Size: 4

## Ingredients 
- pork shoulder, 500g, thinly sliced
- pineapple, 1 small, peeled and sliced
- dried guajillo chillies, 2
- dried ancho chillies, 2
- garlic cloves, 3
- white vinegar, 2 tbsp
- pineapple juice, 3 tbsp
- ground cumin, 1 tsp
- dried oregano, 1 tsp
- salt, 1 tsp
- small corn tortillas, 12
- white onion, 1, finely chopped
- coriander, a handful, chopped
- limes, 2, cut into wedges

## Method 
1. Soak the dried chillies in hot water for 10 minutes until soft, then drain.
2. Blend the chillies with the garlic, vinegar, pineapple juice, cumin, oregano and salt to a smooth paste.
3. Coat the pork slices in the marinade and leave for at least 2 hours (overnight is better).
4. Heat a heavy pan on high and cook the pork in batches until charred at the edges. Don't crowd the pan.
5. Cook the pineapple slices in the same pan until lightly caramelised, then chop into small pieces.
6. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan.
7. Fill each tortilla with pork, top with pineapple, onion and coriander. Serve with lime wedges.

## Serving Suggestions
Good with a cold beer and some salsa verde on the side.
