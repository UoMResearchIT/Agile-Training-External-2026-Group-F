# Churros con Chocolate

- Cuisine Type: Mexican
- Preparation Time: 25 minutes
- Cooking Time: 20 minutes
- Serving Size: 4

## Ingredients

- Water, 1 cup
- Unsalted butter, 2.5 tbsp
- Granulated sugar (for the dough), 1 tbsp
- Salt, 1/4 tsp
- All-purpose flour, 1 cup
- Large eggs, 2
- Vegetable oil (for frying), 3 cups
- Granulated sugar (for coating), 1/2 cup
- Ground cinnamon, 1 tbsp
- Whole milk, 2 cups
- Semisweet chocolate, chopped, 6 oz
- Cornstarch, 1 tsp

## Method (step-by-step instructions)

1. In a medium saucepan, combine the water, butter, sugar, and salt. Bring to a boil over medium heat.
2. Remove from heat and immediately add the flour, stirring vigorously with a wooden spoon until the mixture forms a smooth, thick dough that pulls away from the sides of the pan.
3. Let the dough cool for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it's just warm to the touch.
4. Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition until the dough is smooth, glossy, and elastic.
5. Pour the vegetable oil into a deep, heavy pot and heat to 375°F (190°C). Use a thermometer for accuracy.
6. Spoon the dough into a piping bag fitted with a star tip. Pipe 4-5 inch strips directly into the hot oil, cutting with scissors or a knife.
7. Fry in batches of 4-5, turning occasionally, until deep golden brown and crisp on all sides (about 3 minutes per batch).
8. Remove with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels for a minute. Mix the coating sugar and cinnamon on a plate, then roll the warm churros in the mixture until fully coated.
9. In a saucepan, whisk together the milk and chocolate over medium-low heat until melted and smooth. Dissolve the cornstarch in a splash of cold milk and whisk it in to thicken slightly.
10. Pour the hot chocolate into small cups and serve immediately alongside the warm, sugared churros for dipping.

## Serving Suggestions

Serve immediately while hot and crisp. Pair with a steaming cup of Mexican drinking chocolate (made with chocolate tablets like Abuelita or Ibarra for extra authenticity) or a café de olla for an after-dinner treat. Churros are best eaten fresh, within 15–20 minutes of frying, before they lose their crispness.
