# Healthier Burritos

- Cuisine Type: Mexican
- Preparation Time: 15 mins
- Cooking Time: 50 mins
- Serving Size: 4-6

## Ingredients

**Base**
- Brown rice, 300g
- Wholemeal tortilla wraps, 4-6

**For the chilli**
- Olive oil, 1 tsp
- Beef mince, 400g-500g
- Large onion, finely chopped, 1
- Garlic cloves, crushed, 2
- Chipotle paste, 2 tsp
- Cajun or fajita seasoning, 1 tbsp
- Brown sugar, 1 tbsp
- Cider vinegar, 1 tbsp
- Black beans, drained, 400g can
- Chopped tomatoes, 400g can

**For the guacamole**
- Tomato, chopped, 1
- Red onion, finely chopped, 1
- Coriander, chopped, ½ small pack
- Lime, juiced, 1
- Avocados, stoned and peeled, 2

**To serve**
- Natural yogurt
- Lime halves
- Grated cheddar (optional)

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a frying pan over a medium heat, crumble in the mince and fry for 15-20 mins until the liquid has bubbled away and the mince starts to brown and crisp. Break it up with a wooden spoon so it caramelises all over, then stir in the onion and cook for another 10 mins until softened.
2. Add the garlic, chipotle paste, spice mix, sugar and vinegar, and cook for 1 min. Tip in the beans and tomatoes, stir and simmer uncovered for 20 mins until thickened.
3. Meanwhile, put the rice in a saucepan with 600ml cold water and a pinch of salt. Bring to the boil, then reduce the heat, cover and simmer for 20 mins until the water is absorbed. Turn off the heat and leave undisturbed for 10 mins, then stir and set aside.
4. For the guacamole, mix the tomato, red onion, coriander and lime juice with some seasoning, then mash to a chunky consistency.
5. To serve, warm the tortillas and spread over some guacamole. Add a layer of rice, then the chilli, a dollop of yogurt, a squeeze of lime and grated cheddar if using. Wrap tightly, halve through the middle and serve.

## Serving Suggestions

Serve with extra lime wedges on the side. The chilli keeps refrigerated for two days or frozen for up to a month — defrost thoroughly and reheat until piping hot before serving.
