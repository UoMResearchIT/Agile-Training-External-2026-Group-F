# Spaghetti Aglio e Olio
- Cuisine Type: Italian
- Preparation Time: 5 mins
- Cooking Time: 10 mins
- Serving Size: 4

## Ingredients
- spaghetti, 400g
- garlic cloves, 6
- olive oil, 120ml
- chilli flakes, 1 tsp
- parsley, a handful
- salt, to taste
- pepper, to taste

## Method 
1. Boil a big pot of salted water and cook the spaghetti until al dente.
2. While that's going, heat the olive oil in a pan on low. Add the garlic and chilli flakes, cook for a couple of minutes until golden — don't burn it.
3. Save a mug of pasta water before draining.
4. Toss the spaghetti in the pan with the garlic oil, add a splash of pasta water to loosen.
5. Stir in the parsley, season, serve.

## Serving Suggestions
Good with bread and a green salad.
