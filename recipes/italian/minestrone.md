# Minestrone  

- Cuisine Type: Italian
- Preparation Time: < 30 min
- Cooking Time: 30 min to 1 h
- Serving Size: 4

## Ingredients (clear list with quantities)

- olive oil, 1 tbsp
- onion, 1
- carrot, 2
- celery stick, 3
- garlic, 2 cloves
- tomato puree, 1 tbsp
- chopped tomatoes, 1 tin / 400 g
- vegetable stock, 1.2 l
- canelli beans, 400g
- spagetti, 100g
- green cabbage, 0.25 head
- Salt and pepper

## Method (step-by-step instructions)
1. Chop onion, carrots, celery and garlic
2. Heat olive olive oil, middle heat, add chopped onion, carrots and celery
3. Cook for 10 min under occasional stirring
4. Add garlic and fry for another minute
5. Stir in tomato puree then cook for another 3 min
6. Add tomatoes and stock, cover and bring to boil.
7. Simmer at reduced heat for 15 min.
8. Break the pasta and add, add beans.
9. Cook until pasta is cooked.
10. Add cabbage
11. cook for another two minutes
12. Season with salt and pepper

## Serving Suggestions
Serve with sourdough or garlic bread
